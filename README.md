## About

I am the author of three articles published in the **Romanian Cyber Security Journal (ROCYS)**, the scientific journal of the National Institute for Research & Development in Informatics (ICI Bucharest). My research covers:

- **IoT security** – how Denial-of-Service attacks exploit weakly secured connected devices, and what can be done about it
- **Privacy-preserving cryptography** – Private Information Retrieval (PIR) protocols, which let a user query a database without revealing what was retrieved
- **AI for email security** – adaptive, machine-learning-based detection of phishing campaigns, including AI-generated ones

This repository is my public portfolio. It currently holds my research publications; projects and other work will be added over time.
